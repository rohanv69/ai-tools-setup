# Core Ideas

## 1. People don't care about your product

People care about:

- themselves
- their pain
- their goals
- becoming better versions of themselves

Marketing starts with empathy, not features.

---

## 2. Great marketing is storytelling

Frameworks help.

But deep customer understanding creates:

- positioning
- messaging
- narratives

Storytelling is more powerful than templates.

---

## 3. Brand is one of the last defensible moats

Products get copied.

Features get copied.

Brand reputation and trust are much harder to replicate.

Founder branding strengthens company branding.

---

## 4. Find attention gaps

Don't blindly copy competitors.

Ask:

- What isn't everyone doing?
- What's missing?
- What's being ignored?
- Where can we stand out?

Different beats louder.

---

## 5. Content is the highest leverage marketing asset

Content can:

- educate
- entertain
- build trust
- generate demand
- establish authority

before asking for a sale.

---

## 6. Real feedback matters

Useful feedback:

- DMs
- comments
- audience conversations
- conference interactions
- replies
- qualitative signals

Not everything important lives in analytics dashboards.

---

## 7. Balance short-term and long-term

Short-term:

- sales
- revenue
- survival

Long-term:

- reputation
- brand
- trust
- audience

Strong businesses invest in both.

---

## 8. AI increases the importance of judgment

Everyone will have access to AI.

The differentiators become:

- creativity
- positioning
- storytelling
- taste
- strategic thinking
- judgment

---

## 9. Human relationships become more valuable

As automation grows:

- communities
- in-person events
- trusted people
- authentic relationships

increase in value.

---

# Mental Models

- People don't buy products—they buy better versions of themselves.
- Story beats specifications.
- Marketing is earning attention.
- Brand is accumulated trust.
- Creativity creates differentiation.
- Community compounds.
- AI amplifies execution, not judgment.
- Great content teaches or entertains.
- Real human connection is a competitive advantage.
