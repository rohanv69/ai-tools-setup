## Core Philosophy

- AI is not a tool advantage anymore—how you work with AI is the advantage.
- Prompt engineering and context engineering are becoming foundational knowledge-worker skills.
- The difference between mediocre and exceptional AI output is often the quality of prompting and context.
- Curiosity and repetition are more valuable than titles or credentials in the AI era.

---

## Prompt Engineering

### Prompting is a Competitive Advantage

- There can be a 100x difference in outputs between poor and excellent prompting.
- Prompt engineering should be treated as a craft, not a shortcut.
- Great prompts are often the result of repeated iteration rather than one-shot creation.
- Kieran has spent days refining a single prompt.

### Prompting Principles

- Don't ask vague questions.
- Give examples of what "good" looks like.
- Explain why examples are good.
- Include reference styles and writers.
- Specify desired outcomes instead of generic platform labels.

Example:

Instead of:

> Write me a LinkedIn post about lead generation.

Provide:

- examples
- performance context
- writing style references
- desired tone
- desired outcome

## Context Engineering

Context is often more important than prompting.

Great context includes:

- examples
- background information
- company information
- previous work
- preferred style
- constraints
- objectives

The better the context, the better the AI performs.

## Vibe Prompting

Kieran advocates "vibe prompting":

Instead of manually engineering everything:

- use AI to improve prompts
- use AI to critique prompts
- use AI to generate prompts
- use AI to become better at prompting

AI becomes the prompt coach.

---

## Memory as a Superpower

ChatGPT memory creates long-term leverage.

Examples:

- ask AI how you could use it better
- ask it to improve your historical prompting
- ask it for before/after prompt comparisons
- use accumulated memory as personalized coaching

Memory creates switching costs and compounds over time.

- build workflows
