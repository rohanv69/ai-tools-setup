## Key ideas

### 1. Pattern Interrupt

Break expectations to capture attention.

Predictable content gets ignored.

Unexpected framing increases engagement.

---

### 2. Warm Outreach > Cold Outreach

Don't pitch strangers.

Build relationships first.

Talk to people already engaging with your content.

---

### 3. Signposting

Tell people why they should trust you.

Example:

- "After coaching 1,000 founders..."

- "After 15 years as CEO..."

Authority should be demonstrated naturally.

---

### 4. Pre-Planting

Use comments strategically.

Comment for everyone reading the post.

Don't comment only for the author.

---

### 5. Comments are leverage

Comments often generate more visibility than posts.

Be visible inside conversations.

---

### 6. Psychology drives attention

Content should use:

- Curiosity

- Surprise

- Pattern interrupts

- Human behavior

- Trust

- Authority

---

### 7. LinkedIn should generate inbound opportunities

Content should:

- Build trust

- Build authority

- Generate leads

- Create inbound business

Ideas worth applying:

- Add authority naturally through signposting

- Focus on warm relationships instead of cold outreach

- Build trust before selling

- Make content psychologically engaging
