## Key ideas

### 1. Employee expertise is an unfair advantage

Hands-on employees possess unique first-hand knowledge that AI cannot replicate.

Encourage subject matter experts to publish publicly.

---

### 2. Experience matters

Original experiences create differentiated content.

AI cannot replace genuine lived expertise.

---

### 3. Zero-click marketing

Brands should focus on becoming memorable and discoverable rather than chasing website clicks.

Visibility itself creates value.

---

### 4. AI search rewards authority

Important signals include:

- Expert authors

- Credible sources

- Third-party validation

- LinkedIn

- Reddit

- YouTube

- Industry authority

---

### 5. High-quality content still wins

Journalistic quality, expertise and trustworthy information remain competitive advantages.

---

### 6. LinkedIn newsletters are underutilized

Publishing newsletters directly on LinkedIn can rapidly grow subscribers and visibility.

---

### 7. AI visibility requires ecosystem thinking

Success depends on:

- Website

- LinkedIn

- Reddit

- PR

- YouTube

- Employee advocacy

- Creator partnerships
