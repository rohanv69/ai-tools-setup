# Justin Welsh - Video Research Notes

## Title

Justin Welsh: How to Make $10M with No Team, No Office, No Investors

## URL

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0UxQbhaoMlQ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0UxQbhaoMlQ)

## Speaker

Justin Welsh

## Main topics

- Solopreneurship
- Knowledge monetization
- Content strategy
- Trust building
- Long-form writing
- LinkedIn growth
- Newsletter strategy

## Key ideas

- Give value before asking for sales
- Trust compounds over time
- Educational content outperforms self-promotion
- Build expertise before monetization
- Repurpose content across platforms
- Focus on one platform first
- Long-form builds authority

## Interesting quotes

- "Give, give, give, ask."
- "Consistency compounds over time."
- "Problem solvers earn attention."
- "Create once, repurpose many times."

## My observations

Justin consistently prioritizes trust over aggressive selling. His strategy revolves around educational content, audience understanding, and long-term relationship building rather than viral tactics.

## Possible takeaways for this project

- Strong educational positioning
- Soft CTAs instead of hard selling
- Story-first writing style
- One core content engine with multi-platform distribution
