## About

Ross Simmonds is the founder and CEO of Foundation, a content marketing agency focused on helping B2B and SaaS companies maximize the impact of their content through strategic distribution rather than creation alone. His philosophy centers around putting the "marketing" back into content marketing by emphasizing amplification, partnerships, repurposing, syndication, and multi-channel distribution.

Foundation has worked with leading SaaS companies and publicly traded cloud businesses, executing campaigns that have reached millions across Google, LinkedIn, Reddit, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, and other major platforms.

Ross consistently advocates that creating content is only half the battle. Success comes from building systems that ensure content reaches the right audiences repeatedly across multiple trusted channels.
