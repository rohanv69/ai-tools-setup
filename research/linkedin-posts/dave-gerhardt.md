## Writing Style

- Conversational and self-deprecating

- Writes like he's talking to a friend

- Uses simple language instead of jargon

- Frequently tells personal stories

- Mixes business lessons with family and life anecdotes

- Makes abstract concepts tangible through examples

---

## Common Writing Patterns

### 1. Start with a strong belief

Examples:

- "Nobody cares about your product."

- "Brand is the last moat."

- "Marketing is creativity and storytelling."

The opening immediately creates curiosity or disagreement.

---

### 2. Explain through stories

Rather than theory, Dave almost always explains ideas using:

- Drift

- Privy

- Exit Five

- customers

- conferences

- family

- personal experiences

---

### 3. End with a practical takeaway

Every story ends with a lesson readers can actually use.

---

### 4. Humanize the content

Frequently references:

- wife

- kids

- Vermont

- golf

- coffee

- workouts

- ice cream

- daily routines

- funny observations

This makes the content feel personal instead of corporate.

## Core Marketing Principles

### People don't care about your product

They care about:

- themselves

- their problems

- emotions

- status

- improvement

Marketing should start there.

---

### Story beats features

Narrative creates memorability.

Good stories create:

- tension

- conflict

- heroes

- villains

- transformation

---

### Creativity wins

Don't copy competitors.

Find:

- new angles

- new formats

- new stories

- attention gaps

---

### Brand is a moat

Products get copied.

Brand reputation compounds.

Trust becomes a competitive advantage.

---

### Marketing is attention

Find opportunities competitors ignore.

Different beats louder.

---

### Content should educate or entertain

Useful:

- tutorials

- frameworks

- playbooks

- examples

Entertaining:

- humor

- relatable situations

- memorable stories

---

### Feedback loops matter

Signals include:

- DMs

- comments

- conversations

- conference interactions

- audience replies

Not just dashboards.

---

### Community compounds

The strongest indicator isn't subscribers.

It's members voluntarily recommending you.

---

### AI Philosophy

- Learn AI aggressively

- Don't fear AI

- Taste matters more

- Judgment matters more

- Creativity matters more

- Human relationships matter more
