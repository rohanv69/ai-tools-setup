# Who is Kevin Indig?

Kevin Indig is a Growth Advisor and one of the most respected voices in SEO, Organic Growth, and AI Search. Having led organic growth initiatives at companies like Atlassian, G2, and Shopify, he now advises leading technology companies including Meta, Ramp, hims, and Upwork while publishing the highly regarded Growth Memo newsletter.

Unlike traditional SEO practitioners, Kevin increasingly focuses on the future of search, AI visibility, reputation, and authority. His work explores how brands can remain discoverable in an AI-first internet where citations, mentions, and trust matter as much as website rankings.

## Main Content Themes

### 1. AI Search & AI Visibility

Kevin believes search is fundamentally changing from ranking webpages to synthesizing trusted information.

Recurring ideas:

- AI visibility matters more than rankings
- Citations become a competitive advantage
- Share of voice is replacing traditional SEO metrics
- Reputation influences discoverability
- External authority matters as much as owned media

Representative ideas:

> "Prompt tracking should look more like polling than keyword tracking."

---

### 2. Reputation Over Rankings

One of Kevin's strongest recurring themes is that websites are no longer the sole source of discoverability.

Brands should build authority through:

- LinkedIn
- Reddit
- Reviews
- Industry publications
- Podcasts
- Community discussions
- Third-party citations

His work repeatedly emphasizes ecosystem-wide visibility instead of website-centric thinking.

---

### 3. Original Research & Proprietary Information

Kevin consistently argues that AI commoditizes generic information while increasing the value of unique knowledge.

High-value assets include:

- Original research
- Internal data
- Experiments
- Customer insights
- Frameworks
- Strategic observations

This aligns strongly with founder-led content strategies for B2B SaaS.

---

### 4. Better Measurement Systems

Kevin advocates replacing vanity metrics with more meaningful indicators.

Examples include:

- Share of Voice
- Citation Rate
- Mention Rate
- AI Visibility
- Relative Competitive Position
- Brand Presence
- Market Share

He consistently stresses that every metric should ultimately connect to revenue and business outcomes.

---

### 5. Judgment as Competitive Advantage

As AI lowers execution costs, Kevin argues that strategic thinking becomes increasingly valuable.

He identifies three critical skills:

- Prioritization
- Attention
- Judgment

Technology makes execution easier.

Knowing *what* deserves execution becomes the differentiator.
